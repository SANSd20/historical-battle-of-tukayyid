[size=24pt][b]BATTLE OF TUKAYYID[/b][/size]
[i]Version 0.1 Playtest[/i][/center]

This is a work-in-progress update to the Battle of Tukayyid campaign system. It retains the original campaign's structure and character while incorporating selected rules and concepts from the BattleTech Core Rulebook and Hot Spots: Draconis Reach.

Only changed or additional rules are presented below. Unless specifically stated otherwise, continue using the published Battle of Tukayyid and Hot Spots: Draconis Reach rules, together with either the BattleTech Core Rulebook or Total Warfare and BattleTech: Mercenaries.

All feedback is welcome, whether based on actual play or simply reviewing the rules. Feedback on balance, campaign pacing, clarity, Track objectives, and the campaign economy would be especially helpful.

[quote]In May 3052, the armies of ComStar met the invading Clans on Tukayyid. For twenty-one days, they fought across seven campaign regions to decide whether the Clan advance toward Terra would continue. ComStar prevailed, and the resulting truce halted the invasion for fifteen years.

At the time, Tukayyid was celebrated as the battle that saved the Inner Sphere. Its defenders had denied the Clans their ultimate prize and given the Great Houses time to recover, rearm, and prepare for the conflicts that followed.

Yet, as the battle's centennial approaches, Terra rests in Clan hands and an ilClan rules from humanity's birthplace. Tukayyid unquestionably changed the course of history, but its victory came at a terrible price.

Was the time purchased on Tukayyid worth its cost—and what did the Inner Sphere ultimately do with it?

[i]—Dr. Elara Venn, Tukayyid: A Century Later, Tukayyid Memorial Institute, 3151[/i][/quote]

Use either the BattleTech Core Rulebook or the combination of Total Warfare and BattleTech: Mercenaries for the core game and Battlefield Support rules. Battle of Tukayyid and Hot Spots: Draconis Reach are required with either rules path. Any applicable published rule not addressed here remains in effect.

[size=18pt][b][color=#213854]1  CAMPAIGN FRAMEWORK[/color][/b][/size]

[b]Campaign Structure. [/b]Retain the Battle of Tukayyid Regions, force construction, campaign sequence, and original-owner recovery framework.

[b]Orders. [/b]Orders objectives are not used in this campaign.

[b]Objective Points. [/b]Objectives award Objective Points (OP). OP determine the winner of the current Track and are then discarded; they never carry into another Track.

[b]Strategic Points. [/b]Strategic Points (SP) are the campaign's only persistent resource. Warchest Points are not used. The side that wins the most Regions wins the campaign.

[b]Repair Category. [/b]Use only the highest repair category that applies to a unit. After determining that category, apply all Technology Base and unit-type modifiers, then round fractions up.

[b]Availability. [/b]Armor-only repairs, rearming, and reconfiguration are ready for the next Track. Structure, critical, crippled, and destroyed-unit repairs are unavailable until the next Region. Replacement units are also unavailable until the next Region.

[b]Reconfiguration. [/b]A unit must be fully repaired before reconfiguration.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Replacement and Recovery[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Replacement Slot. [/b]Only a destroyed unit creates a replacement slot. The replacement must be the same broad type: BattleMech, battle armor, combat vehicle, or conventional infantry. Weight class, model, and variant may change if the replacement is legal and affordable.

[b]Selection. [/b]Select a replacement from the relevant faction or Tukayyid Random Assignment Table. Its pilot uses the skill level purchased for that roster slot. A replacement begins with full standard ammunition.

[b]Recovery Roll. [/b]After a Track, the original owner rolls 2D6 for each eligible destroyed unit. On a result of 9+, the unit is recoverable. On a result of 8 or less, it is lost. Conventional infantry, crashed aerospace units, and units destroyed by the final damage from artillery or bombing cannot be recovered.

[b]Truly Destroyed. [/b]A BattleMech is truly destroyed when its center-torso structure is eliminated. A vehicle is truly destroyed by a fuel-tank explosion or by elimination of internal structure in a non-turret, non-rotor location. Battle armor uses a 7+ unit-survival check. A truly destroyed unit cannot be recovered or repaired.

[b]Ownership. [/b]Enemy units are never captured. Every recoverable unit remains the property of its original owner.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Personnel[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[li]A vehicle crew is killed when its vehicle is destroyed.[/li]
[li]Each Commander Hit or Crew Stunned result inflicts one wound on the affected crew member.[/li]
[li]Each battle armor trooper survives destruction on 7+; Clan battle armor applies a −2 modifier to this target number.[/li]
[/list]

[b]Designation. [/b]Each Star or Level II may designate one named commander. Named status is permanent. Unnamed personnel may act as ordinary formation leaders but gain no named benefits.

[b]Initial Commander. [/b]An initial named commander uses the unit's purchased skill level, begins with Edge 1, and receives 150 advancement SP. The 150 SP may buy Gunnery, Piloting, Edge, or Edge Abilities, but not Special Command Abilities.

[b]Promotion. [/b]An eligible unnamed pilot may be formally promoted only between Tracks, after casualties are resolved and before the next Track force is selected. A promoted commander retains current skills, begins with Edge 1, receives no free advancement SP, and becomes permanently named.

[b]Unnamed Personnel. [/b]Unnamed pilots do not improve skills or Edge. They may record Commendations for promotion priority.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Advancement[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Campaign Spending. [/b]After each Track, a force may divert up to 200 SP per Campaign Scale from its Support Allocation to participating named commanders, with a maximum of 100 SP assigned to any one commander.

[b]Commendation Bonus. [/b]A named commander who receives that Track's Commendation gains 20 additional advancement SP.

[b]Edge. [/b]At the start of each Track, each named commander's Edge refreshes to its current rating; unspent Edge never carries over. After rolling, spend 1 Edge to add +1 to an attack roll, reroll a motive-damage result, or reroll a critical hit scored against that commander's unit. Edge and Edge Abilities belong to the commander and cannot be transferred.

[b]Excluded Systems. [/b]Do not use the Draconis Reach handicap/BSP compensation system or unmatched Special Command Ability initiative rules.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Formation and Command[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Formation Training. [/b]Formation training is organizational and free. Assign it through the parent Level III or IV, or the applicable Clan Binary, Trinary, or Cluster, based on formation type. It may change when assignments change between Tracks. It has no SP cost or delay.

[b]Special Command Abilities. [/b]Retain the existing Tukayyid Combatant Special Command Abilities. No commander may buy, learn, or replace an SCA during this campaign.

[b]Acting Leader. [/b]If a formation commander is killed, destroyed, or withdraws, appoint any surviving pilot or unit commander in that formation as Acting Leader. The Acting Leader does not inherit Edge, Edge Abilities, or training. A named Acting Leader retains only their own benefits.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Commendations and Retirement[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Commendation. [/b]Each side awards one Commendation after every completed Track, regardless of result. The recipient must have participated and survived. A surviving pilot whose unit was destroyed or who ejected remains eligible. The player chooses the recipient.

[b]Promotion Priority. [/b]When a named slot is vacant, the eligible unnamed pilot with the most Commendations has priority. Break ties by player choice. If no eligible pilot has a Commendation, choose any eligible pilot. Commendations are permanent and are not spent.

[b]Retirement. [/b]Between Tracks, a player may permanently retire a pilot. Retirement is irreversible and all development is lost. Retiring a named commander grants a free new named commander with the retired unit slot's purchased skills and Edge 1, but no inherited Commendations, advancement, Edge Abilities, or starting 150 SP.

[size=18pt][b][color=#213854]5  TRACK RULES[/color][/b][/size]

[b]Track End. [/b]Unless a Track states otherwise, check every Track End condition during the End Phase. An objective ends a Track only when its rules specifically say so.

[b]Published Values. [/b]Retain every published objective value that this document does not expressly replace. Ignore all Orders objectives.

[b]Recovery. [/b]Apply the campaign-wide original-owner recovery rules even when the Draconis Reach version of a Track prohibits salvage.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Assault[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Conquer. [/b]Completing Conquer does not end the Track. If both sides complete it in the same End Phase, neither side scores it.

[b]Track End. [/b]During the End Phase, the Track ends if either side has no non-crippled units in play or the turn limit has been reached: Turn 10, or Turn 8 in the shortened format.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Breakthrough[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Objective Sequence. [/b]Completing either preliminary objective does not end the Track.

[b]Track End. [/b]During the End Phase, the Track ends if one side completes Hold the Field, either side has no units in play, or the turn limit has been reached: Turn 10, or Turn 8 in the shortened format.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Meeting Engagement[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Make Their Acquaintance. [/b]The first side to cripple or destroy one-third of the opposing force's BV scores this objective. If both sides reach the threshold in the same End Phase, neither scores it. Voluntary withdrawals count toward the threshold.

[b]Force Points Option. [/b]If Force Points are used instead of BV, use a 50% threshold.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Flank[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Defender Emplacements. [/b]The Defender receives two Veteran Medium Emplacements per Campaign Scale. Each emplacement may instead be replaced by up to 20 BSP of emplacement Assets. Place them in the Attacker's half of the battlefield.

[b]Attacker Entry. [/b]One-third of the Attacker's force enters from its home edge on Turn 1. The remainder enters from a short edge on Turn 2.

[b]Thresholds. [/b]Crush and Turn the Tide each use a 50% threshold.

[b]Cut Off Retreat. [/b]Maintain either one non-crippled BattleMech or two Battlefield Support Assets per Campaign Scale near the Defender's home edge for two consecutive End Phases.

[b]Fall Back. [/b]After Turn the Tide is completed, withdraw at least 50% of the eligible force.

[b]Ramming Speed. [/b]Add the Ramming Speed objective using its Draconis Reach procedure. It is worth 150 Objective Points.

[b]Track End. [/b]End when either side has no units in play or at Turn 8 (Turn 6 for the shortened format).


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Pushback[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Vehicle Detachment. [/b]The Attacker receives a separate vehicle-only detachment: 12/24/36 BSP in Original mode or 32/64/96 BSP in Modern mode. This detachment is in addition to normal Battlefield Support.

[b]Entry. [/b]The primary force enters from its home edge on Turn 1. The vehicle detachment enters from the side edges on Turn 1, halfway toward the Defender's edge.

[b]Objectives. [/b]Use Push; Crush at one-third; Gutted at 75%; Advance to the Rear instead of Hold Ground; Make Them Hurt at 50%; and the updated Lead objective. Remove Cut Off the Head and ignore Orders.

[b]Track End. [/b]End when either side has no units in play or at Turn 10 (Turn 8 shortened). After Turn 6, completing Advance to the Rear also ends the Track after the End Phase.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Pursuit[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Roles. [/b]Determine momentum by the campaign roll. The Defender flees and the Attacker pursues; the role assignment is not fixed by faction.

[b]Entry. [/b]The Defender enters from its home edge on Turn 1. The Attacker enters from the same edge on Turn 2.

[b]Force Construction. [/b]Use the Tukayyid equal-force construction and campaign recovery rules.

[b]Fleeing Force. [/b]At least half of the Defender's force must have a maximum movement of 8 or less (16 inches or less in Alpha Strike). Temporary damage does not make a unit qualify.

[b]Objectives. [/b]Qualifying units count for Escape and Never Here.

[b]Track End. [/b]End when either side has no non-crippled units in play or at Turn 10.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Recon[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Defender Deployment. [/b]Use the restricted Defender deployment from Draconis Reach.

[b]Scan. [/b]The Attacker must scan two-thirds of the required targets. Preemptive Strike uses a 25% threshold.

[b]Observe and Report. [/b]Use Observe and Report in place of Escape. Withdrawal may begin after Turn 4.

[b]Deny. [/b]Score Deny proportionally. Defender units that withdraw early count as scanned or destroyed, as applicable.

[b]Track End. [/b]End when either side has no units in play or at Turn 8.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Retreat[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Entry. [/b]The Defender retreats from the Attacker's home edge toward its own. The Attacker enters from that same edge on Turn 2.

[b]Scan. [/b]Use a two-thirds scan threshold.

[b]Hammer. [/b]The first side to reach 25% scores Hammer. If both reach it in the same End Phase, neither scores.

[b]Gauntlet. [/b]Complete Gauntlet when half of the eligible force escapes; crippled units count.

[b]Track End. [/b]End when either side has no units in play or at Turn 10.


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Strike[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Objectives. [/b]Retain the Tukayyid objectives.

[b]Battlefield. [/b]Use the Draconis Reach building durability and deployment zones. Retain the Tukayyid fortress option.

[b]Reinforcements. [/b]Replace the secret 1D6+1 arrival with guaranteed Turn 2 flank entry.

[b]Track End. [/b]End when either side has no units in play or at Turn 10 (Turn 8 shortened).


[size=14pt][b][color=#607FAA]Supply[/color][/b][/size]
[b]Track. [/b]Retain the Tukayyid Supply Track. Do not add Objective Raid as a separate Track.

[b]Warehouses. [/b]Place six buildings and secretly identify two as warehouses. A warehouse must be scanned before loading.

[b]Components. [/b]Each warehouse holds one component per Campaign Scale, for a total of 2/4/6 components.

[b]Handling. [/b]Use the Draconis Reach loading, carrying, transfer, and movement-penalty rules for components.

[b]Objectives. [/b]Retain the Tukayyid casualty and survival objectives.

[b]Delayed Group. [/b]The delayed group enters on Turn 6.

[b]Track End. [/b]End when either side has no units in play or at Turn 10.

[b]Campaign Reward. [/b]Each component carried off the Attacker's home edge awards the Attacker 500 SP. This reward is in addition to the Track's normal Support Allocation.
