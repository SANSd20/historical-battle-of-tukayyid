# Playtesting Historical: Battle of Tukayyid

Feedback based on actual play or rules review is welcome. The first priority is a complete **Star-scale Region**, with particular attention paid to the **Flank** and **Supply** Tracks.

## Before play

- Record the rules version, game system, campaign scale, Region, and Track.
- Record which faction is the Attacker and which is the Defender.
- Record each side's deployed BV or PV and relevant skill adjustments.
- Record starting SP and the Original or Modern BSP mode.
- Note any optional rules, proxies, substitutions, or house rules.

## During the Track

- Record the turn on which each objective is completed.
- Record the OP earned by each side.
- Note which rule ends the Track and the ending turn.
- Record major Battlefield Support effects, especially emplacements.
- For Flank, record whether Ramming Speed is attempted and completed.
- For Supply, record each component located, loaded, transferred, lost, and recovered.
- Note any rule that interrupts play because its meaning is unclear.

## After the Track

- Record the result and final OP totals.
- Record each side's Support Allocation and other SP awards.
- Record repair, recovery, replacement, and ammunition spending.
- Record surviving, crippled, destroyed, and permanently lost units.
- Note whether either side felt compelled to pursue or ignore an objective.
- Note whether the Track ended too early, too late, or at an appropriate point.
- Note whether bookkeeping affected the pace of play.

## Campaign-level review

After completing a Region, compare each side's:

- SP gained and spent.
- Remaining effective Campaign BV.
- Available replacement slots.
- Named commander advancement and Commendations.
- Battlefield Support use.
- Ability to continue without an unrecoverable advantage or deficit.

## Submitting feedback

Use the repository's GitHub issue forms:

- **Actual-play report** for a played Track or Region.
- **Balance feedback** for values, costs, rewards, or structural concerns.
- **Rules clarification** for unclear or conflicting text.

One report per Track is ideal. A single Region summary may be added after its individual Track reports.
